About this space

Welcome to our cozy home! This private room with a secure entrance is part of a shared basement apartment, equipped with a kitchen (no stove) and a clean shared bathroom. The room includes a Full-sized bed, a Smart TV, and a reach-in closet. Everything else outside the room — kitchen, bathroom, common area — is shared.

We have friendly dogs that guard the property and alert us. Street parking is available nearby. You're just 12 minutes from restaurants, nightlife, and the marina.

There is a long-term tenant residing in the final room of this basement apartment; this tenant will not interfere with your stay.
